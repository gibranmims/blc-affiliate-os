## 1. Product overview

**BBL Serum** (Bright Bikini Line Serum) is a lightweight daily skincare serum
formulated to improve the appearance of ingrown hairs, razor bumps, dark spots,
post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation, uneven skin tone, and rough skin texture on
the bikini line and other external body areas affected by hair removal or friction.

Unlike products that focus on only one concern, BBL Serum combines gentle
exfoliation, skin-brightening ingredients, and soothing hydration in one formula.

It is designed for gradual improvement through consistent use rather than immediate
results. **External use only.**

- **Bottle size:** 1.7 fl oz / 50 mL
- **Cruelty-free:** Yes — the finished product is not tested on animals.
- **Vegan:** Yes, vegan-friendly.
- **Fragrance:** Formulated without added fragrance or parfum.
- **Alcohol:** Formulated without ethyl alcohol or Alcohol Denat.

> **Claim-wording rule.** Use the exact phrasings above. Do not upgrade
> "the finished product is not tested on animals" into a broader ingredient-level
> claim. Do not substitute the bare phrase "alcohol-free."

## 2. How the formula works

BBL Serum combines four ingredient groups.

### Exfoliating support
- Salicylic Acid
- Willow Bark Extract

Help reduce dead skin buildup and support the appearance of fewer trapped hairs
and smoother texture.

### Brightening support
- Kojic Acid
- Alpha-Arbutin
- Sodium Ascorbyl Phosphate
- Niacinamide
- Licorice Root Extract

Work through different cosmetic pathways to help improve the appearance of dark
marks and uneven tone over time.

### Soothing and antioxidant support
- Aloe Vera
- Green Tea Extract
- Centella Asiatica
- Licorice Root Extract
- Niacinamide

Help support hydration, comfort, and overall skin condition.

### Hydration and formula support
- Glycerin
- Propanediol
- Butylene Glycol
- Water

Keep the serum lightweight, spreadable, and hydrating.

Because skin renews gradually, improvements happen over weeks, not days.

## 8. How to use

1. Clean the area.
2. Allow skin to become **completely dry**.
3. Dispense 1–2 pumps.
4. Apply a thin, even layer.
5. Allow it to absorb naturally.
6. Wash hands afterward.

Do not rinse off. **Nighttime use recommended.**

### Frequency
- **Beginners:** 3–4 nights per week, increasing gradually.
- **Experienced:** once nightly.

More frequent application will not produce faster results.

### Hair removal timing
Wait **at least 48 hours** after shaving, waxing, sugaring, laser, IPL, or
electrolysis. Applying too early increases irritation risk.

### Layering

**Avoid:** glycolic acid, lactic acid, mandelic acid, other salicylic acid
products, chemical exfoliating pads, strong exfoliating toners.

**Compatible:** gentle cleansers, simple moisturizers, sunscreen.

Hydrating body oils are best used separately rather than immediately after the serum.

### SPF
The formula contains actives that encourage skin renewal, so treated skin may
become more sensitive to sunlight. Apply sunscreen whenever treated skin will be
exposed to UV. Sun protection prevents additional pigmentation and supports better
long-term brightening results.

## 10. Suitability

### Who it's for
Adults experiencing ingrown hairs, razor bumps, bikini-line discoloration,
underarm discoloration, inner-thigh friction, uneven tone, or rough texture after
hair removal. **Suitable for all skin tones and all genders.**

**Men:** Yes. The formula works on external skin regardless of gender. The brand
markets primarily toward women, but the formula is appropriate for anyone with
ingrown hairs or discoloration.

### Who should not use it
Do not use if skin is broken, actively irritated, sunburned, cut, or has an active
infection — or if the customer knows they are allergic to an ingredient.

Never apply internally, on mucous membranes, or around the eyes.

### Allergy and sensitivity
Customers should not use BBL Serum if they know they are allergic or sensitive to
any listed ingredient. Because the formula contains salicylic acid, customers with
a known salicylic acid or aspirin sensitivity should check with a qualified
healthcare professional before use.

**The agent must not diagnose a salicylate allergy or tell a customer the product
is definitely safe for them.**

### Sensitive skin
Fragrance-free and formulated without ethyl alcohol or Alcohol Denat. For sensitive
skin: patch test first, start slowly, increase only if tolerated. If irritation
develops — stop, let skin recover, restart only once fully healed. Persistent
irritation should be evaluated by a healthcare professional.

### Pregnancy
Contains active exfoliating and brightening ingredients. Pregnant customers should
discuss use with their physician or OB-GYN before adding it to their routine.
**The Bikini Line Co. does not provide medical clearance.**

### Breastfeeding
Should also consult a healthcare provider before introducing products containing
active ingredients.

### Under 18
Should consult a dermatologist or healthcare provider before use. Patch testing
recommended.

## 13. Common mistakes

Customers see slower results when they:

- Apply immediately after shaving
- Apply to damp skin
- Use too much product
- Layer multiple exfoliating acids
- Stop after one or two weeks
- Skip sunscreen
- Use the product inconsistently

## 14. Troubleshooting

**"It isn't working."** Check: How long have they used it? Applying nightly? Is
skin completely dry? Did they wait 48 hours after hair removal? Using other
exfoliating products? Exposing the area to sun without protection?

**"It's stinging."** Stop use. Rinse with cool water if needed. Allow skin to
recover. Restart gradually only if fully healed.

**"I used too much."** A thin layer is sufficient. More product will not improve
results faster and may increase irritation.

**"I missed a few days."** Resume the normal schedule. Do not double up.
