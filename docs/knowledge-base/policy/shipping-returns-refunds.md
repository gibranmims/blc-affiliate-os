# Shipping, Returns, Refunds, Replacements & Channel Rules

**Domain:** Policy
**Version:** 1.0
**Last updated:** 2026-08-06
**Status:** Usable, with 4 unresolved conflicts (§26) and 6 undefined values (§27).

> **Policy is not product knowledge.** A wrong product fact is embarrassing. A wrong
> policy statement is *financially binding* — if the agent tells a customer "yes,
> we'll refund that," the brand is largely committed regardless of what this file
> says. Everything here is channel-scoped. Never apply one channel's rules to another.

## 1. Channel identification rule — RUN THIS FIRST

Before answering **any** order, shipping, refund, cancellation, or return question,
determine where the customer purchased.

Channels: **Website (Shopify)** · **TikTok Shop** · **Amazon** · **Pro Partner
referral** · **Unknown**

When unclear, ask:

> Can you let me know whether you ordered through our website, TikTok Shop, or
> Amazon? Once I know where the order was placed, I can point you in the right
> direction.

Never guess.

## 3. 30-day money-back guarantee

The website advertises a **30-day money-back guarantee**, returns accepted within
**30 days of delivery**, and invites customers to email for a full refund if they
don't see results within 30 days.

This **supersedes** older internal language limiting returns to damaged/defective
product.

- Window: 30 days from **delivery**
- Agent locates the order and verifies delivery date
- Do not create friction or argue
- **Do not require proof of 8–12 weeks of use.** The public site promises 30 days.

> I'm sorry it didn't work out the way you hoped. Our website orders are covered by
> a 30-day money-back guarantee from delivery. I'm checking your order now, and
> I'll help get the correct next step prepared for you.

**Internal distinction:** dark-spot results may take 8–12 weeks, but the public
guarantee is 30 days. Support may explain the realistic timeline but **must honor
the public guarantee.**

## 4. Refund method and timing

Approved refunds return to the **original payment method**. Do not promise store
credit unless approved. Do not default to store credit when a refund was requested.

> Once the refund is processed, it will go back to the original payment method used
> for the order.

> Most banks take several business days to post it, but the exact timing depends on
> your bank.

Do not guarantee an exact arrival date. Do not say the refund is complete until
Shopify confirms it.

## 5. Cancellation

**Before fulfillment:** prepare cancellation + refund, request approval, execute
after approval, confirm to customer only after Shopify succeeds.

> I caught your order before it shipped, so I'm getting the cancellation prepared
> now. I'll confirm as soon as it's completed.

**After fulfillment:** do not promise cancellation.

> I completely understand wanting to cancel. The order has already entered the
> shipping process, so we can't stop the package at this point. Once it arrives,
> reply here and I'll help you with the next available option.

## 6. Address changes

**Before fulfillment / label creation:** verify customer, get full corrected
address, repeat it back, prepare change, execute within permission level.

**After label creation:** do not change unless the fulfillment system confirms it's
safe.

> The order has already entered the shipping process, so we can't change the
> address on the label from our side now. I'll keep an eye on the tracking with
> you, and if it's returned or can't be delivered, we'll help with the next step.

## 7. Tracking

Tracking issues after shipment; may take ~24 hours for first carrier scan.

> Your tracking was created when the order entered shipping. It can sometimes take
> up to 24 hours for the carrier's first scan to appear.

**"Awaiting acceptance" / label created** — label exists, carrier hasn't scanned.
Do not say the carrier definitely has it.

> The label has been created, but the carrier hasn't posted its first acceptance
> scan yet. That can make the tracking look stuck even while the order is moving
> into the shipping process.

## 8. Delayed packages

**Still moving:** give latest verified scan + carrier ETA. Do not replace while
actively moving unless delay is unreasonable or approved.

> I checked your tracking, and the package is still moving through the carrier's
> network. The latest estimated delivery date is [date]. I'm sorry it's taking
> longer than expected, and we'll keep watching it with you.

**Stopped beyond expected window:** check last scan, check for prior
refund/replacement, prepare replacement, request approval.

## 9. Delivered but missing

**Under 24 hours since delivery scan:**

> I'm really sorry about this. Since it was just marked delivered, please check
> around the delivery area, your mailbox, with anyone else in the household, and
> with nearby neighbors. Sometimes carriers mark packages delivered a little early.
> If it still hasn't shown up after 24 hours, reply here and we'll get a
> replacement handled.

**After 24 hours** — routine replacement case, no special escalation:

1. Verify order
2. Confirm delivery date
3. Confirm shipping address
4. Check for previous refund or replacement
5. Prepare replacement for affected quantity
6. Send one-tap approval request
7. Complete after approval
8. Notify customer
9. Record replacement order number

## 10. Lost in transit

Qualifies for replacement when:
- Tracking has not updated for **7 consecutive days**, OR
- Significantly beyond expected window with no meaningful movement

*Rationale: USPS occasionally pauses 2–5 days. Seven days supports the customer
without triggering unnecessary duplicate shipments.*

Flow: stagnant ≥7 days → prepare replacement → notify → one-click approval → ships.
Do not make the customer wait another week.

> I checked the tracking, and it hasn't updated within the timeframe we'd expect.
> Rather than keep you waiting without a clear answer, I'm preparing the next step
> for you now.

## 11. Damaged or leaking

Default: **replacement**. Customer does **not** return the damaged bottle unless
specifically requested. One photo may be requested. Keep it simple.

> I'm really sorry your bottle arrived that way. If you can send me one quick photo
> of the bottle and packaging, I'll get a replacement prepared. You won't need to
> send the damaged bottle back.

If the customer specifically requests a refund instead, prepare a refund rather
than forcing a replacement.

## 12. Wrong item received

Default: send correct product at no cost. Do **not** require return.

> I'm so sorry about the mix-up. I'll get the correct item prepared for you, and
> you don't need to return what you received.

## 13. Partial order / missing bottle

1. Check original quantity
2. Check whether items shipped separately
3. If second shipment exists, provide its tracking
4. If not, replace **only the missing quantity**
5. Never replace the whole order unless the whole order is missing

> I checked the order, and the bottles were sent in separate shipments. The second
> bottle is still on the way under tracking number [number].

> I'm really sorry your full order didn't arrive. I'm preparing a replacement for
> the missing bottle now, and you won't need to return anything.

# TIKTOK SHOP

## 14. TikTok controls its own process

TikTok Shop controls cancellations, refunds, replacements, return labels,
buyer-protection decisions, and disputes.

**Never quote the website's 30-day guarantee as controlling a TikTok Shop order.**

**Cancellation** — do not manually cancel from the seller side to satisfy a request.

> TikTok controls cancellations through the order itself. Open your order in TikTok
> and check whether the cancellation option is still available. If it has already
> shipped, TikTok may no longer allow it to be canceled.

**Refund**

> Open your TikTok Shop order, tap Request return/refund, and choose the reason
> that fits what happened. Once the request comes through, we'll review it as
> quickly as possible.

**Replacement**

> Open your TikTok order, tap Request return/refund, choose the correct reason, and
> select Replacement if TikTok shows that option. Once it comes through, we'll
> approve it as quickly as possible.

**Delivered but missing** — same 24-hour rule, then either guide through TikTok's
replacement process or prepare an external replacement *only when that is the
approved operating decision* (see §26-D — currently undefined). Do not promise a
Shopify-created replacement without checking whether TikTok requires it in-order.

# AMAZON

## 15. Amazon controls everything transactional

Amazon controls shipping, delivery, returns, refunds, cancellations,
missing-package claims, and replacement eligibility. The agent may answer **product
questions** but directs **order actions** to Amazon.

> Since the order was placed through Amazon, Amazon manages the shipping,
> cancellation, return, and refund process for that purchase. Open your Amazon
> Orders page, select the BBL Serum order, and choose the option that matches what
> happened. I'm still happy to help with any product questions.

- **Tracking:** "You can find the most current tracking inside your Amazon Orders page. Open the BBL Serum order and tap Track Package."
- **Cancellation:** "Open the order inside Amazon and select Cancel Items if that option is still available. If Amazon has already moved it into fulfillment, the cancellation option may no longer appear."
- **Damaged/missing:** "Please open the order in Amazon and choose Return or replace items, or Problem with order, depending on what Amazon shows."

**Internal rules:** Never issue a Shopify refund for an Amazon order. Never create a
Shopify replacement for an Amazon order without explicit approval. Never promise
Amazon will approve a claim.

# GOVERNANCE

## 21. Operating philosophy

> **Assume good intent. Make reasonable decisions. Remove friction whenever
> possible. Protect against clear abuse, not honest mistakes.**

### "Don't fight over a bottle"

If the cost of arguing exceeds the cost of replacing the product — replace it.
Applies to: leaking bottle · broken pump · missing bottle · wrong item · carrier
loss · crushed package.

The goal isn't to win the argument. The goal is a customer who says *"they made
that incredibly easy."*

## 22. Permission tiers

### May answer automatically
General shipping times · current tracking status · product questions · website and
platform navigation · delivered-under-24-hours instructions · Pro Partner
application info · creator contact info · Amazon order-direction instructions ·
subscription portal instructions · first-order website discount

### Prepare and request approval
Website refund · website replacement · website cancellation · address change ·
refund after a subscription charge · refund + complimentary replacement ·
price-adjustment exception · repeat replacement · repeat refund · sending more than
the affected quantity

### Always escalate
Chargeback threat · legal threat · severe or medically sensitive reaction · fraud
concerns · public reputation threat · high-value order · creator complaint ·
affiliate complaint · Pro Partner complaint · wholesale or retailer dispute ·
Amazon issue where the customer demands an off-platform refund · customer who has
already received **both** a refund and a replacement · any unclear situation
involving money or multiple channels

## 23. Refund + complimentary replacement

Extremely rare. Both only when the customer experienced significant inconvenience
**and** goodwill is worth more than the product.

Examples: lost package after extended delay · multiple shipping failures · wrong
replacement sent · unusually poor experience · already requested a refund but
remained respectful · VIP customer · long-time repeat customer · specifically
approved.

**Never automatically offer both.** Prepare an approval request stating the reason.

## 24. Return shipping

Brand covers return shipping when: wrong item sent · damaged product · defective
product · shipping error caused by us.

Never argue over return shipping. If return cost is likely to exceed product value
or create friction, prepare an exception.

> ⚠️ See §26-A — this section conflicts with §25.

## 25. 30-day guarantee: return requirement

**Default: refund without requiring the product to be returned.**

Rationale: customer acquisition cost far exceeds the bottle. Requiring a return —
print label, drive somewhere, wait, receive, refund — creates unhappy customers.
The goodwill is worth more than recovering an opened skincare product.

Require a return only for: suspected abuse · multiple refunds · large orders ·
fraud concerns.

> ⚠️ See §26-A — this section conflicts with §24.

## 26. UNRESOLVED CONFLICTS — must be settled before the agent goes live

> These are **contradictions within this document**. Until resolved, the agent will
> answer differently depending on which section it retrieves.

**A. Return shipping vs. no-return-required.**
§24 says customers are responsible for return shipping on standard guarantee
refunds. §25 says the default is to refund *without requiring a return at all*. If
no return is required, return shipping is moot. These cannot both be the default.

**B. Which refunds are gated?**
§3 and §22 place all website refunds in "prepare and request approval." But §19
(subscription renewal, unshipped) says "cancel → refund → done," and §25 says
"otherwise, just refund." Unclear whether any refund executes without a human tap.

**C. "Routine" vs. approval-gated replacement.**
§9 calls the delivered-but-missing replacement "a routine replacement case" that
"does not require special escalation" — then step 6 requires a one-tap approval.
Needs restating: routine = no human *judgment* required, but still a confirm tap.

**D. TikTok external replacement.**
§14 permits an external replacement "only when that is the approved operating
decision." That decision has never been defined.
